# Impeccable — Scorer

## Design Context

### Users
Momentum Netball club volunteers on the bench: one person scoring, one recording
stats for their own team, each on their phone, courtside in a bright sports hall,
glancing between live play and the screen. Sessions last a 40–60 minute match.
Secondary: a coach reading the match report afterwards.

### Brand Personality
Quick, loud, dependable. Momentum Netball club app — violet is the club colour
(the legacy app was indigo/purple). It should feel like sports equipment: sturdy,
legible at arm's length, zero ceremony.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light theme (bright halls, daytime use), very high contrast, scoreboard-utilitarian.
Display face: Saira Condensed (tall, athletic, numeric). UI/body face: Chivo.
Violet accent (OKLCH), neutrals tinted toward violet; green = made/won,
red = missed/conceded as semantic colours only. Anti-reference: generic admin
dashboard, thin grey text, small buttons.

### Design Principles
1. The scoreboard is the interface — the most important number is the biggest thing on screen.
2. Every action is one thumb-tap and gives instant visible feedback; corrections are always one tap away.
3. Nothing critical is ever behind a modal or a scroll during live play.
4. The action log is the source of trust — everything recorded is visible and undoable.
5. Works when the venue wifi doesn't: capture never blocks on the network.
